Identifies your requests to the National Weather Service, for example WBNS.

The National Weather Service asks automated tools to include a way to reach their owner. Use a shared newsroom or weather-team address.

For example WBNS-TVDC-2. Only used to write the network addresses in your setup notes.

Record dots

Pulls the National Weather Service lists of locations forecast to be at or near a daily record and turns them into dots for Max. The preview below shows your icons large, then at roughly on-air size.

Bottom strip: the same icons at about 28 pixels, scattered the way a busy day looks.

Current look matches the red, orange and blue dots in use today. Distinct shapes also gives each record type its own shape, which helps color-blind viewers.

Dots

The ring keeps dots readable on both light and dark maps. Without it, each dot gets a thin dark outline instead.

Each refresh downloads 28 small lists from the National Weather Service.
